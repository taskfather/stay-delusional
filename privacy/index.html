Privacy Policy

Effective 11 September 2026 · For App Store review and users of Stay Delusional

This policy describes how Stay Delusional (“we”, “the app”, “this site”) handles information. The product is a daily identity ritual on iPhone. It is not a social network, not a health record, and not advertising-funded.

Who this applies to

The Stay Delusional iOS app and this website (including GitHub Pages at taskfather.github.io/stay-delusional and staydelusional.com when it points here). If you only visit the marketing pages, we do not run ads or sell lists. GitHub Pages may process standard server logs as described in GitHub’s own privacy statement.

What the app stores on your device

The ritual lives on the phone. We store locally, among other things:

  • The name and identity sentence you type in onboarding
  • Journal text you write
  • Streak, day progress, and the house state
  • Notification preferences for local reminders

This content is not uploaded to our servers. Deleting the app deletes it from the device. We cannot recover a journal from our side.

What we receive on our servers

The app sends product analytics so we can see whether the ritual is finishing or dropping mid-guide. Events go to a database hosted by Supabase. Each event may include:

  • A one-way hash of an install identifier (not your name, Apple ID, or email)
  • Event name (for example open, guide started, guide finished, journal finished, day complete, streak lost)
  • Calendar day, streak number, guide pack/variant, slide index, and durations in milliseconds

We do not collect journal text, the identity sentence, your real name, precise location, contacts, photos, payment data, or an advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not run third-party ads. We do not sell personal information. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

The app also fetches a public content catalog (guide copy) from this website or a public CDN. That request is not tied to your journal.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on the device through Apple’s notification system. We do not send marketing push from our servers.

Children

Stay Delusional is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete server-side event data we can reasonably identify.

Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies: we process analytics under legitimate interests in understanding whether the product works, in a form that is not meant to identify you. On-device ritual data is processed because you asked the app to keep it. You may object to analytics by not using a build that phones home, or by contacting us as below.

Processors

  • Supabase, Inc. — hosts the events database
  • GitHub, Inc. — hosts this website and the public catalog
  • Apple Inc. — App Store, device OS, and (after release) standard App Store analytics available to us as the developer

Those companies process data under their terms. We do not use the analytics to profile you for ads.

Retention

On-device data stays until you delete it or the app. Server events are kept to operate and improve the product. We delete them when they are no longer needed, or sooner if you ask and we can match the records.

Your requests

You can access, correct, or delete data on the device inside the app or by deleting the app. For server events: we cannot look you up by name. Write to us via the Support page. If you include an approximate install date or any install hash shown to you, we will delete matching rows where reasonably possible.

EEA/UK users may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. EU users can also contact us for GDPR requests at the Support address.

Not medical or financial data

Stay Delusional is a mindset ritual. It is not a health, therapy, diagnostic, or trading product. Do not put information in the journal that you need kept to a clinical or legal standard we do not offer. The journal never leaves the device through our analytics.

Changes

If we add an account, cloud sync, or paid features that change this picture, we will update this page and the App Store privacy labels before that version ships. Using a newer version after the date above means you see the then-current policy.

Contact

Privacy and data requests: Support.
