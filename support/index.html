Support

This is the support URL for App Store Connect.

Stay Delusional is a daily identity ritual on iPhone: read the guide, write one line, keep the streak. There is no account and no in-app chat.

How to reach us

Open a note on GitHub (public, fastest today):

github.com/taskfather/stay-delusional/issues

Or email: office@taskfather.ai

For privacy or deletion requests, put “Privacy” in the subject. We do not receive your journal. Analytics are stored under a one-way install hash, not your name — include an approximate install date if you have it.

Common

  • The house is gone. The streak ends at midnight if the guide and a non-empty journal were not both done. That is the product, not a bug.
  • I deleted the app. Local journal and streak are gone. We cannot restore them.
  • Notifications. iPhone Settings → Stay Delusional → Notifications.
  • Not in the App Store yet. Correct. This site is the product page until the listing is live. There is no public download here.

Safety

If you are in immediate danger or thinking of harming yourself, do not use this form as help. In the US call or text 988. In the EU call 112. Use local emergency services.
